Cardiovascular + stroke together kill over 15 million people per year — more than cancer, diabetes, and respiratory disease combined. They account for roughly 32% of all global deaths.

The steepest regional growth in dementia is projected in North Africa / Middle East (+367%) and Eastern sub-Saharan Africa (+357%) between 2019–2050 — driven by rapidly ageing populations with little existing care infrastructure.

💀

Suicide claims 700,000 lives per year globally. Men account for 75% of suicide deaths. Suicide is the 4th leading cause of death among 15–29 year olds. Depression raises cardiovascular disease risk by 40%.

👶

Youth mental health crisis: Mental disorder burden among under-24s accelerated sharply after 2019, with DALY rates increasing 6.64% per year. Anxiety and depression have surged across all adolescent populations globally.

💊

Treatment gap is enormous. In low-income countries, more than 75% of people with mental disorders receive no treatment. Even in high-income countries, the treatment gap is 40–50%. Stigma and cost are the primary barriers.

Cardiovascular services generate 21.5% of all global hospital revenue — the single largest segment. This reflects both disease prevalence and the high cost of cardiac procedures, stents, bypass surgeries, and long-term management.

🏥

Public vs Private split: Globally, public/community hospitals held the largest market share (36.6% of beds) in 2024. Inpatient services account for 58% of revenue. Outpatient services are the fastest-growing segment due to telemedicine and day procedures.

📈

China is the fastest-growing hospital market — expected to reach $818B by 2030. Meanwhile, healthcare spending growth (5.8% CAGR) is consistently outpacing GDP growth (4.3%) in most OECD nations — an unsustainable long-term trend.

Greece's core health challenges: CVD and cancer account for 65% of all deaths. High smoking rates (27%), widespread obesity (65% overweight), and a shift away from the Mediterranean diet are the primary drivers. Premature CVD mortality under 65 is significantly above the EU average.

💊

Healthcare structural gap: Greece has the highest doctor density in the EU (6.1/1,000) but one of the lowest nurse densities (3.5/1,000 vs EU avg 8.8). The system over-invests in secondary care vs primary care, and rural areas face severe access inequalities.

Key Global Health Milestones

Major turning points in the modern epidemiological transition

🦠
1980s

HIV/AIDS Pandemic Begins

HIV/AIDS emerges as a global health crisis, becoming the 7th leading cause of death by 2000. By 2021, treatment advances brought deaths down 61% — one of medicine's greatest achievements.

📈
2000

Non-Communicable Disease Era Begins

The WHO Global Health Estimates baseline. Cardiovascular disease already the world's #1 killer. Dementia deaths begin their steep ascent. Diabetes prevalence doubles over the next two decades.

🫁
2019–2021

COVID-19 Reverses Decades of Progress

COVID-19 became the 2nd leading cause of death globally in 2021 (1.2M directly attributed). It reversed life expectancy gains of 5+ years in some countries and disrupted cancer screenings, causing secondary mortality effects still being measured.

🧠
2021

Dementia Deaths Triple Since 2000

Alzheimer's and dementias responsible for 1.8M deaths — nearly 4× more than in 2000. Now 4th leading cause of death in high-income countries. No cure. Cost: $1.3 trillion annually. By 2050: 139 million affected.

💉
2024

Diabetes Surpasses 800 Million

WHO confirms over 800 million adults now live with diabetes — a 4× increase since 1990. Combined with obesity affecting 1 in 8 globally, the metabolic health crisis is accelerating faster than any intervention can counter.

🤖
2025–2030

AI & Genomics Begin to Transform Care

AI projected to save $150B+ annually in healthcare by 2026. Early cancer detection, personalised medicine, and predictive cardiology are all emerging from research into clinical application — though access remains deeply unequal.
